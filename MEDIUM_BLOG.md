### Analytics That Actually Tell You Something

This is where most people's jaws drop.

Every click on every link you manage is tracked with full context:

- **Where in the world** is your audience? (Country-level geographic breakdown)
- **What device** are they on? (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet)
- **Which browser** do they use? (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
- **How did they find you?** (Direct, Search, Social, External referrers with domain detail)

### Three Levels of Link Privacy — Built for Real Life

Not every link should be public. All in1 URL has a three-tier privacy system that no competitor has thought through this carefully:

🟢 **Public** — Shows on your hub, visible in search results. Perfect for your main platforms.

🟡 **Unlisted** — Accessible to anyone with the direct link, but hidden from your public hub. Perfect for sharing with specific people without cluttering your page.

🔴 **Private** — Password-protected. Share only with people you hand the password to. Perfect for client resources, team links, or anything sensitive.

One platform. Three privacy modes. **Total control.**

Don't want the noise? Turn each notification type off independently. Weekly performance summary emails are also available if you prefer a digest instead of real-time pings.
